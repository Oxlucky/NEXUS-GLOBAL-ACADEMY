Are you Giving Trading what it wants

Are you giving trading what it really wants?

You want to become a profitable trader. You want to make money from the market.

But ask yourself: Are you giving the market what it wants from you?

If you’re not willing to respect the process, build discipline, and stay consistent, then how can you expect the market to make you profitable?

Stop keeping yourself in a delusion.

Don’t keep telling yourself, “It’s just a matter of time. One day I’ll have enough experience, and I’ll become profitable.”

Time alone won’t change anything.

If you keep doing the same shit for the next 10 years, you’ll still be exactly where you are today.

Nothing changes until you change.

The market wants discipline.
The market wants consistency.
The market wants patience.

And if you can’t give it those things, then stop dreaming about the results.

Wake up. Face reality. And start giving trading what it actually demands.
